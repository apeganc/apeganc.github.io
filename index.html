Yummy – Favorite Dish Notifier

Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, HTMX, Docker

Yummy is a small project I built to learn more about web development and the tech stack I wanted to use.

It started as a simple web app with one job: let me know on the days my favorite dish is on the menu at General Hospital Celje. (Trust me, as hospital canteens go, it’s one of the best in Slovenia.) Since then, it has grown into a small platform that can watch different restaurants, five so far. If your lunch place isn’t on the list yet, you can write to me, and if Yummy can read the menu, I’ll add it.

Try it at yummy-notifier.crunchy.si. More details in the README.

Clinical Studies Tracker (demo)

Next.js, React, MongoDB, Better Auth, Vercel

A demo of a tracker for clinical studies: sites, status and CRA (Clinical Research Associate) assignments. Feel free to sign up and try it out. Everything resets to the sample data every night.

I made it for a problem I dealt with in my own work. Most clinical study systems are built by the sponsor, for the sponsor, so if you run studies for five sponsors, you end up with a portal for each one and a folder of spreadsheets holding it all together.

It’s built to give managers a clear overview: every study in one list, down to each recruiting site with its status and notes. Filter by sponsor, protocol or CRA to quickly see who is assigned to what, and keep everything up to date as study or site details change.

At work I also saw how overwhelmed people in healthcare sometimes are by all the systems they first have to learn, in clinical trials especially. This tracker is designed to be easy to use and based on actual needs in the Contract Research Organization (CRO) space.

The demo is at app-clinical.crunchy.si. More details in the README.

Amielo (new!)

Next.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Tailwind CSS, Stripe

Amielo is my favorite project – a cute web app for creating personalized digital gifts. The giver picks a template, like breakfast or movie night, tailors the menu, then adds a personal touch with a recorded voice message and animations. The recipient opens a private link, chooses what they’d like from the menu and receives a confirmation card with a calendar invite. After the event they can create a memory card to remember it by.

The digital part sets up the surprise and makes it feel personal, while the experience itself happens offline.

Opening soon at amielo.app. When it does, buying a gift is the nicest way to support me :) More details in the README.
